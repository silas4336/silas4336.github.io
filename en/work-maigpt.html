Project · Feb – May 2026

MaiGPT

The company's enterprise AI platform, used by enterprise customers adopting AI and the admins who manage it. I owned front-end product decisions and their implementation.

First the advanced features: prompt template management, conversation sharing, and the knowledge-base setup flow. Creating a knowledge base used to start from several different places; I moved it into one, with empty-state guidance and an automatic return once you are done. The template library later went through a full round of improvements.

Then the AI Store rebrand and Skills integration: a reworked skill taxonomy, layered display, and the page hierarchy cut from six levels to three. Plus an overview tab and a redesigned filter.

Specs as well as features: two PRDs, for conversation sharing and the prompt template library. For conversation sharing I also wrote a reduced and a minimal version — the full scope turned out too large to ship, and only the minimal one actually fit into a cycle. The product spec sheet I rewrote after finding the previous version had been written from documents rather than from using the product: I clicked through every screen in the admin and wrote it again.

Built with AI-assisted development (Claude Code).
